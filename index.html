File Galaxy

Demo galaxy
Runs entirely in your browser locally: nothing is being sent over the network, no data is collected, and none of your files are being uploaded anywhere. Folders are read gradually with live progress, and you can also drag a folder (or everything inside a drive) onto this window. If your browser only offers the classic picker (or you Shift+click the button), a huge drive may pause the page for a while before anything appears: that is the browser itself listing the files, it is not frozen. You can save the “mapped” data file of your drive or a folder, so you can load the generated galaxy instantly, instead of doing a full re-scan. Enjoy!

Troubleshooting
If a scan crashes the browser, the console is lost with it. So while a scan runs, the app keeps a small record of it in this browser. When you open the app again it tells you if the last scan didn’t finish and where it got to, and you can export the log. In Chrome or Edge you can also have the log written to a file every couple of seconds while scanning: save it somewhere other than the drive you are scanning (your Desktop, for example). Logs list folder paths from your scan, so look them over before sending them to anyone. A scan that finishes normally leaves no paths in the saved record.
